Babur Museum

This is the birthplace of the great ruler and poet Zahiriddin Muhammad Babur. He is not only important for Andijan but also for the entire history of Uzbekistan.

Jome Masque

One of the largest mosques in the city, known for its beautiful architecture and peaceful atmosphere.

Old City Bazar

If tourists want to experience the true spirit of Andijan, they should visit this market, where they can find local products, fresh fruits, and handmade crafts.

A wonderful place to relax and enjoy nature with family and friends.
